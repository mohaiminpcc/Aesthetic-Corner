About the artist

Asma Binte Nurul Huda

Asma Binte Nurul Huda is a professional artist with 12 years of experience in painting. She works in two styles: photo-realistic paintings that look like photographs, and abstract paintings built from colour and shape.

She paints in acrylic and watercolour. Each painting is handmade, so each piece is the only one of its kind.

To see more photos of a painting, or to talk about colours and size, message us on WhatsApp.
